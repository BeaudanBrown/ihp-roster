## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information in connection with our software platform, onboarding, customer support, exports and related services.

## 3. The kinds of personal information we may collect

Depending on the service and your relationship with us, we may collect:

- identity and contact details, such as name, email address and phone number
- account and login information
- business and venue account information
- worker profile information entered by customers or workers
- roster, leave, availability and timesheet information
- support and communications records
- subscription billing status and Stripe-hosted billing metadata
- export, audit and activity information
- technical information such as IP address, device and browser information, and application logs

We do not intend to collect sensitive information unless it is reasonably necessary for the service and specifically supported by our product and processes.

## 4. How we collect personal information

We may collect personal information:

- directly from you
- from your employer or business
- when an authorised customer creates or manages user or worker records
- when you use our platform
- when you contact us for support
- through service providers involved in hosting, email, analytics, logging or security

## 5. Why we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information

We may use personal information to:

- provide and manage the platform
- onboard customers and users
- manage roster, leave, timesheet and export workflows
- provide support and troubleshooting
- maintain system security and auditability
- comply with legal obligations
- improve and administer our services

## 6. Disclosure of personal information

We may disclose personal information:

- to the relevant customer business and its authorised users
- to workers in connection with their own access and records
- to service providers who help us host, operate, support or secure the platform
- to Stripe where Stripe-hosted payment or subscription billing services are used
- to professional advisers
- where required or authorised by law

Where supported by the platform and authorised by the customer, information may also be exported or disclosed to payroll staff, bookkeepers or accountants.

## 7. Overseas disclosure

We aim to use Australian hosting and Australian-default service providers where reasonably practical.

However, some service providers or technical services may involve overseas handling or disclosure. Where that is the case, we will describe the relevant arrangements in our service documentation or subprocessor information.

## 8. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

These steps may include:

- access controls
- venue-scoped permissions
- encryption in transit
- secure hosting and backups
- audit logging
- restricted production access

Payment method details, such as card or bank account details, are handled by
Stripe-hosted billing pages. We do not intend to collect or store those payment
method details in the platform.

## 9. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless longer retention is required or permitted by law.

Some employment-related and payroll-adjacent records may need to be retained for longer periods to support customer legal obligations, including record-keeping requirements.

## 10. Access and correction

You can request access to personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us using the details above.

In some cases, we may need to work with the relevant customer business to respond to the request.

## 11. Complaints

If you have a privacy complaint, contact us first using the details above.

We will review your complaint and respond within a reasonable period.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our practices or services change. The current version will be made available through our website or customer materials.
