# Customer Terms Draft

## Status

Commercial draft for tailoring and legal review before use with paying customers.

## 1. Parties

These terms apply between:

- `[Business name]` (`Provider`)
- the customer business identified in the order, signup or proposal (`Customer`)

## 2. Services

The Provider supplies access to workforce administration software and related support services, including rostering, timesheets, leave workflows and related export functions as agreed.

## 3. Customer account and venue

- The Customer receives a venue environment or account within the service.
- The Customer is responsible for its authorised users.
- The Customer must ensure only authorised persons access the service.

## 4. Customer responsibilities

The Customer is responsible for:

- the lawfulness of the data it inputs or causes to be input
- assigning appropriate user roles
- maintaining the accuracy of employment and payroll-related information
- determining which third parties, including accountants or bookkeepers, are authorised to receive exported data
- reviewing outputs before relying on them for payroll, compliance or accounting purposes

## 5. Provider responsibilities

The Provider will:

- provide the service with reasonable care and skill
- provide agreed onboarding and support services
- take reasonable steps to protect service data
- maintain backup and operational procedures appropriate to the current service stage

## 6. Data handling

- The service stores and processes workforce and operational data on behalf of the Customer.
- The Provider may access customer data where reasonably necessary to provide onboarding, support, troubleshooting, maintenance or security services.
- Any such access should be limited, documented and auditable.

## 7. Exports and third-party recipients

- The service may allow authorised users to generate exports.
- The Customer is responsible for deciding whether an accountant, bookkeeper, payroll provider or other recipient is authorised to receive exported data.
- The Provider is not responsible for how exported data is handled after it is downloaded or otherwise provided to an authorised recipient, except to the extent required by law.

## 8. Record integrity

- The service is intended to support accurate record-keeping.
- The Customer remains responsible for meeting its employment, payroll and record-keeping obligations.
- The Customer must review and validate records, approvals and exports before external reliance.

## 9. Fees and payment

- Fees are charged per customer venue unless a written order states otherwise.
- Launch subscription pricing is AUD 100 per venue per month.
- Payments are processed through Stripe-hosted payment and billing pages.
- The Provider does not collect or store customer card or bank account details
  in the service.
- The Provider is not registered for GST at launch. Amounts are not GST
  inclusive and Stripe invoices should not be treated as tax invoices unless
  the Provider later confirms GST registration and updated invoicing terms.
- Late, failed or cancelled payment may result in notices to the Customer and
  may result in manual service restriction after reasonable operational review.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party must keep the other party’s confidential information confidential and only use it for the purposes of the relationship, except where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to obtain professional advice.

## 11. Privacy

The Provider will handle personal information in accordance with its privacy policy and applicable law.

The Customer warrants that it has authority to provide personal information to the Provider for the purposes of the services.

## 12. Security incidents

If the Provider becomes aware of a security incident affecting customer data, it will take reasonable steps to investigate, contain and respond to the incident and notify the Customer as appropriate.

## 13. Suspension and termination

Either party may terminate the service on reasonable notice or immediately for material breach.

On termination:

- access to the service may be disabled
- the parties should agree on a reasonable export or handover process
- retained data may continue to be held where required by law, backup cycles or legitimate operational needs
