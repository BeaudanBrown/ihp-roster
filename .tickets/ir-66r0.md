# Migrate duplicated section-style surfaces

Convert surfaces that currently model repeated sections/tabs as separate fragment kinds where parameterization is a better fit.

## Design

Use the inventory to migrate applicable Profile, Staff, Admin/Xero, and other duplicated section/tab/list families. Preserve permissions, visibility, targets, and validation-local behavior. Do not force static one-off fragments into parameterized form where it adds no clarity. Pause and ask if superficially similar fragments have different resources/permissions or if a migration is much larger than expected.

## Acceptance Criteria

All inventory-approved duplicated section families are parameterized. Non-applicable fragments have documented reasons for remaining static. Actor refreshes use resources instead of manual target ids where applicable. Focused tests for migrated surfaces pass.


## Notes

**2026-07-09T01:33:15Z**

User decision: do not parameterize section groups that are not materially the same kind of fragment. Parameterization remains applicable when fragments are homogeneous repeated regions that differ mainly by a typed key/section, or where the section key is the natural resource boundary.

Final classification for duplicated section-style candidates:
- Profile/Staff sections: not applicable for now. Details, preferences, security, leave, and RSA have different dependencies, forms/actions, validation/direct response behavior, and resource semantics. Keeping distinct fragments is clearer than hiding these differences behind profile-section { section }.
- Admin Xero sections: not applicable for now. Shell, staff mappings, pay items, and timesheets have distinct resources and response seams; parameterizing would mostly introduce a mapping table without simplifying behavior.
- Admin Shift Types/Admin Roster Groups: not applicable for now. They are analogous list-management surfaces, but not repeated fragments within one surface; they have distinct actions/resources and should stay separate.
- Support award-rates/public-holidays: not applicable for now. They are two distinct operational panels with distinct resources; low-value to parameterize.

No production code migration is needed for this ticket after the clarified applicability rule. The already-migrated applicable groups are leave section count/list fragments, timesheet day sections, roster day/row fragments, and SurfaceLab panel fixtures.
