# Provision Bepis observability dashboards and trace log correlation

Add initial Grafana dashboards and conventions for navigating between production traces and logs.

## Design

Provision dashboards for Bepis request traces, error logs, roster render hot paths, and diagnostic profile runs. Ensure trace ids appear in logs where practical and Grafana can correlate Tempo traces with Loki log streams.

## Acceptance Criteria

Grafana has provisioned Bepis dashboards; operators can move from a slow/error trace to related logs; labels remain low-cardinality and PII-safe.
