# Refine simple live surface authoring

Make descriptor-first live surfaces easier to add and modify by adding fragment builders, current-venue scope helpers, broader manifest derivation, cookbook examples, and guardrails.

## Design

Layer ergonomic helpers on the existing descriptor architecture without hiding routes, authorization, or resource dependencies. Migrate simple surfaces incrementally and keep complex surfaces on explicit definitions/descriptors.

## Acceptance Criteria

Simple current-venue surfaces can be declared without hand-writing scopeFromWire/default/decorate/empty-interaction boilerplate; descriptor-backed manifest coverage expands; docs and guardrails explain and enforce the easy path; typecheck, frontend checks, and focused Hspec pass.
